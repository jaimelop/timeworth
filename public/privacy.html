Privacy Policy

TimeWorth — Last updated: August 18, 2026

Summary: TimeWorth is a privacy-first, client-only application. We do not collect, store on servers, track, or sell any of your personal or financial data. Everything stays on your local device.

1. Information We Collect

TimeWorth does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or financial account details. The app does not require account registration, login credentials, or access to external banking systems.

2. Local Storage and Data Retention

All calculations, custom settings (such as currency preferences, hourly wage calculations, and custom item comparisons), and calculation history are stored strictly within your browser's or device's localStorage.

  • Your data never leaves your device.
  • We do not operate backend servers that receive, log, or process your calculations.
  • Clearing your browser cache or app storage will permanently remove your locally saved data.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

TimeWorth does not use third-party tracking scripts, telemetry, advertising networks, or user behavioral analytics SDKs.

4. App Permissions

When installed via Android (Trusted Web Activity) or PWA, TimeWorth requires zero special device permissions (no access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or file system storage beyond standard web storage).

5. Children's Privacy

Because TimeWorth does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it complies with the requirements of COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR regarding children's data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make any changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any future updates will continue to honor our commitment to user privacy and local-only data processing.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/jaimelop/timeworth.
